For decades, Carnival's VIFP Club rewarded you for nights sailed. On June 1, 2026, it was replaced by **Carnival Rewards**, a two-part program that rewards spending as well as sailing.

## Two currencies: points and stars

**Points** are earned on what you spend — your cruise fare and onboard purchases. You can redeem them for things like specialty dining, spa treatments, activities and future cruises.

**Stars** determine your status. There are four tiers:

1. Red
2. Gold
3. Platinum
4. Diamond

Status is earned over a two-year window and kept for the following two years.

## What happened to my VIFP status?

- Existing VIFP status carries over as your entry point into Carnival Rewards, valid through **May 31, 2028**.
- **Diamond** members keep Diamond status through **May 31, 2032**.
- Old "Fun Points" balances were converted into Carnival Rewards points at launch.

## The credit card

There's an optional Carnival Rewards Mastercard (issued by Barclays) that earns extra points and can speed up status. Like any co-branded card, it only makes sense if you'd pay it off monthly and cruise Carnival regularly.

<Callout type="money" title="Who wins, who loses">
Travelers who book balconies and suites, buy drink packages and spend on board come out ahead. Frequent cruisers who book cheap inside cabins and spend little on board earn status more slowly than under VIFP.
</Callout>

## How to make the most of it

- **Consolidate spending on your account.** Onboard purchases count, so put your group's extras on the account that's chasing status.
- **Pre-book add-ons.** Pre-cruise purchases are often discounted, and they still count as spending.
- **Mind your dates.** If you held VIFP status, it carries through May 2028 — plan your sailings so you build new status before it expires.

<Verdict>
Carnival Rewards favors people who spend, not just people who sail often. If you were a low-spend VIFP regular, enjoy your grandfathered status through 2028 and decide then whether chasing the new tiers is worth it.
</Verdict>
