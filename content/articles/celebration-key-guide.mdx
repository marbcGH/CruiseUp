Celebration Key opened on Grand Bahama in July 2025, and it's now the centerpiece of Carnival's Bahamas and Eastern Caribbean itineraries. When the new Carnival Festivale debuts from Port Canaveral in May 2027, every one of its sailings will call here.

It's big, it's built from scratch, and a little planning makes the day much better.

## How it's laid out

You arrive at a central hub with shops, bars and food. From there the destination splits into themed areas, each with its own lagoon, beach and food. The key split for most travelers:

- **A family lagoon area** — splash zones, slides and lots of loungers. Best for kids.
- **An adults-only lagoon area** — quieter, with its own swim-up bar.
- **Pearl Cove Beach Club** — a paid, adults-only club with cabanas and premium service.

## What's included, and what isn't

Your fare covers the beaches, lagoons, loungers and the included food venues. Pearl Cove, cabanas, some activities and alcohol cost extra. If you have Carnival's CHEERS! drink package, check the current rules before you go, since packages and private destinations have their own terms.

<Callout type="tip" title="Mind the walk">
The destination is spread out. If you're traveling with young kids or anyone with limited mobility, pick your area before you get off the ship and head straight there rather than wandering.
</Callout>

## Is Pearl Cove worth it?

Pearl Cove makes sense if you want guaranteed shade, fewer people and drinks brought to you. For families or anyone happy with a regular lounger, the included areas are genuinely good. Most people don't need to upgrade.

## A simple game plan

1. **Get off early.** The first hour is the best time to get prime loungers.
2. **Pick one area and stay.** You'll enjoy it more than trying to see everything.
3. **Eat lunch early or late** to avoid the noon rush.
4. **Head back an hour before all-aboard.** The walk and the security line take longer than you think.

<Verdict>
Celebration Key is one of the best reasons to book a Carnival Bahamas cruise. Pick your area in advance, get off early, and skip the upgrade unless shade and service are worth real money to you.
</Verdict>
