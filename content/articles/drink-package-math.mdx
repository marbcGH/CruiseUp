Drink packages are the biggest optional purchase on most cruises, and the one people regret most often, in both directions. Here's how to decide in two minutes.

## The break-even formula

1. **Find the real daily price.** Take the package price and add the gratuity (usually around 18–20%).
2. **Divide by the average drink price.** Cocktails at sea often cost $12–$16, beer $8–$10, specialty coffee $4–$6.
3. **That's your break-even.** If the result is 6, you need to drink six paid drinks every day to come out ahead.

<Callout type="money" title="Example">
A $90/day package plus 18% gratuity is about $106. At $14 per cocktail, you need about 7–8 drinks per day, every day, to break even.
</Callout>

## The rules that catch people out

- **Everyone in the cabin usually has to buy it.** Most lines require all adults in a cabin to purchase the alcoholic package if one does.
- **Every day counts.** You pay for the whole cruise, including port days when you're ashore.
- **Caps exist.** Carnival's CHEERS! package has a daily cap on alcoholic drinks.
- **Private islands can have their own rules.** Check before you assume your package works ashore.

## When it's worth it

- You'll have coffee, sodas and 5+ cocktails or beers a day
- You want the convenience of never looking at a bill
- You found a pre-cruise sale (these happen often)

## When to skip it

- You drink mostly at dinner
- Your itinerary has lots of long port days
- You're on a short cruise where embarkation and disembarkation days eat into the value

<Verdict>
Do the break-even math before buying. If your number is above what you'd honestly drink, skip it. A soda or coffee package might be the better buy.
</Verdict>
