Embarkation day sets the tone for the cruise. Most of the stress is avoidable with a simple plan.

## Before you leave the hotel

- **Check in online** and choose an early arrival time.
- **Pack a day bag**: documents, medications, swimwear, sunscreen, chargers and anything you can't be without for a few hours.
- **Tag your checked bags** with the luggage tags from the line's app or email.

## At the terminal

1. **Hand off your checked bags** to the porters outside.
2. **Go through security and check-in.** Have your passport and boarding pass ready.
3. **Board and get your keycard** if you don't already have one.

<Callout type="tip">
Cabins often aren't ready until early afternoon. Don't wait around. Head straight to lunch or the pool.
</Callout>

## Your first afternoon

- **Eat lunch away from the buffet.** Most ships have a quieter dining venue open at lunch on day one.
- **Book anything you didn't pre-book**, such as shows and specialty dining.
- **Do your muster check-in** before the deadline.
- **Try the rides and slides** while lines are short.

## Before dinner

- **Check your bags have arrived.** If one hasn't arrived by evening, tell guest services.
- **Watch the sail-away** from an upper deck. It's one of the best moments of the trip.

<Verdict>
Arrive early, carry what you need for the afternoon, and spend those first hours enjoying the ship instead of waiting for your cabin.
</Verdict>
