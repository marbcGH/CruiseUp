Great Stirrup Cay was NCL's first private island and, for years, its most criticized: tender boats, crowds and dated facilities. That's changing fast.

## The new pier

A permanent two-ship pier has been under construction since early 2025. Tendering resumed in April 2026 while construction continued, and docking was expected back around late summer 2026. A pier means no tender lines and much easier access for everyone.

<Callout type="warn">
Construction schedules slip. If walking straight off the ship matters to you, check your sailing's latest port information before you go.
</Callout>

## What's new on the island

- **Great Tides Waterpark** — a planned water park with 19 slides.
- **A 1.4-acre pool area** — a huge new pool zone.
- **Vibe Shore Club** — an adults-only area with premium seating and service.
- **Great Life Lagoon** and upgraded dining and bar venues.

NCL's target is to have the upgrades fully operational by the end of 2026, ahead of 2027 sailings.

## Drink packages are back

Earlier plans would have stopped honoring drink packages on the island. NCL reversed that: Free at Sea and More at Sea packages will be honored on Great Stirrup Cay for 2027 guests. [How Free at Sea works](/articles/ncl-free-at-sea-explained).

## Should you choose a sailing because of it?

If you're booking for 2027, yes, it's now a reason to pick an NCL Bahamas itinerary. Fifteen NCL ships are scheduled to visit, so you'll have plenty of options, including the new Norwegian Luna from Miami.

<Verdict>
Great Stirrup Cay is going from NCL's weak spot to a real selling point. For 2027, it's worth choosing an itinerary that includes it.
</Verdict>
