MSC often has the lowest fare on a brand-new ship sailing from Florida, and that makes a lot of cruisers curious. It's a different experience from the American lines, and that's not a bad thing once you know what to expect.

## The ships

MSC's World-class ships are the headliners: World Europa, World America (built for US cruisers and sailing from Miami) and the new World Asia, which launches in the Mediterranean for winter 2026–27. Next up, MSC World Atlantic will sail from Port Canaveral starting winter 2027–28.

The Seaside and Meraviglia classes are also modern and often cheaper.

## How fares work

MSC sells experiences, not just cabins. The names vary by market, but the basic idea is the same: the cheapest tier gets you a cabin with fewer choices, while higher tiers add things like cabin location choice, flexibility and perks.

<Callout type="money" title="Compare totals, not base fares">
A higher experience tier can cost less than the cheapest fare plus the extras you'd buy anyway. Price it both ways.
</Callout>

## The MSC Yacht Club

The Yacht Club is a private ship-within-a-ship: its own pool deck, restaurant, lounge and butler service. During promotions it can cost not much more than a regular balcony on another line. If you want a luxury experience on a big ship, it's one of the best values at sea.

## What feels different

- **Dining is more structured.** Expect assigned times in the main dining room on most fares.
- **The crowd is international.** On European sailings, announcements are in several languages. US-based ships are more English-first.
- **Some things cost extra** that you might expect to be included elsewhere. Buy drink and Wi-Fi packages before you sail; they're usually cheaper.

## The private island

Ocean Cay MSC Marine Reserve in the Bahamas is on most Florida itineraries. MSC is extending its pier so two ships can dock at once, and a second island, Little Cay, is in development next door.

<Verdict>
MSC is a great choice if you want a new ship for less, or real luxury at a good price in the Yacht Club. Go in expecting a European rhythm and buy packages before you sail.
</Verdict>
