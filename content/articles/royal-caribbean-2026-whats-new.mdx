Royal Caribbean had a big 2026. If you last cruised with Royal a couple of years ago, here's what's different.

## Legend of the Seas joins the Icon class

Legend of the Seas, the third Icon-class ship after Icon of the Seas (2024) and Star of the Seas (2025), set off on her maiden voyage from Europe in July 2026. She repositions to Fort Lauderdale for her first North American season, which starts in November.

Two more Icon-class ships are on order, so expect the class to keep growing through 2027–2028.

<Callout type="tip">
A new ship's first few months often have the most hiccups and the highest fares. Sailings 6–12 months after debut usually balance price, polish and novelty best.
</Callout>

## Royal Beach Club Paradise Island

Royal's first beach club opened in Nassau in December 2025. It's a separate concept from Perfect Day at CocoCay: a beach day built around a ship call in Nassau, rather than a private-island stop. A second beach club in Cozumel, Mexico, is slated for late 2026.

**What it means for you:** Nassau used to be a "stay on the ship" port for many repeat cruisers. On ships that include the beach club, it's now a real beach day.

## West Coast moves

- **San Diego:** Serenade of the Seas starts Royal's first San Diego homeport season in October 2026.
- **Los Angeles:** Quantum of the Seas returned for summer 2026, and Ovation of the Seas sails from LA for winter 2026–27.

That's more West Coast capacity than Royal has had in years, so look for competitive Mexican Riviera fares.

## Refits

Ovation, Harmony and Liberty of the Seas each spent roughly a month out of service in early 2026 for upgrades. If you're comparing older Royal ships, recently refurbished ones often feel much closer to new.

## Should you book Royal now?

If you want an Icon-class ship, the Fort Lauderdale arrival of Legend of the Seas adds supply, which can help fares on all three Icon ships. If you're flexible, compare Icon pricing against the Oasis class: you get most of the big-ship experience for noticeably less.

<Verdict>
Legend of the Seas and the new beach clubs are the headline changes. For value, look at West Coast sailings and Oasis-class ships while attention is focused on the newest Icon ship.
</Verdict>
